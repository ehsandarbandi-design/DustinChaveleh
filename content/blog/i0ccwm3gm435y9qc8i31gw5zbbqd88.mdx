## Why Single-App Searches Fail

If you feel like homes go under contract before they even show up on your app, you aren't imagining it. The days of a single website displaying 100% of market inventory are over.

Local MLS boards, major brokerages, and tech platforms compete heavily over listing data. Between "Coming Soon" statuses, office exclusives, and syndication delays, search portals no longer update at the same speed. To catch everything, you need the right tool for the right job.

## The Bigger Trend: "Walled Gardens" & Private Listings

The online search landscape is shifting fast. Major brokerages are actively acquiring tech platforms and expanding **private listing networks** or "office exclusives." Their goal? Keep properties inside their own network for as long as possible before sharing them with the public MLS.

This means more homes are being bought and sold before they ever touch a public app. To get the full picture, relying solely on public aggregators isn't just slow—it leaves you blind to an entire category of off-market inventory.
