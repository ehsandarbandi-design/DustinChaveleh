Building Height **239 Feet** (23 Stories)

Total Units **201 Homes** 30 Affordable BMR

Density Increase **+89%** Over Base Zoning

The **San Francisco Planning Department** has officially granted final approval for a 239-foot residential tower at **1965 Market Street**. The project marks a major milestone in Upper Market development, evolving from an earlier two-building proposal into a singular, high-impact high-rise.

### A Dual-Era Architectural Hybrid

Designed by **RG Architecture**, the 237,000-square-foot structure takes a unique approach to historic preservation. The base incorporates the existing Spanish Revival façade before transitioning into a sleek, modern glass-and-steel tower above.

### Project Breakdown

-   **Residential Mix:** 201 units spanning studios to 3-bedroom layouts.
-   **Affordable Housing:** Includes 30 deed-restricted below-market-rate (BMR) units.
-   **Amenities & Retail:** Ground-floor retail storefronts and an underground garage with capacity for up to 62 cars.

> "Developers across San Francisco are signaling strong market confidence—utilizing family zoning and state density bonuses to expand project scales across the city."
