Duboce Triangle is one of San Francisco’s most charming and centrally located neighborhoods, known for its historic Victorian buildings, leafy streets, and proximity to transit, parks, and vibrant nearby districts. The housing stock is dominated by classic multi-unit buildings—many converted into condominiums—along with a limited number of single-family homes.

#### **Home Values in Duboce Triangle**

Recent sales data suggests the neighborhood continues to command strong prices despite broader market fluctuations. As of 2026:

-   **Median home sale price:** about **$1.37M – $1.42M**

-   **Median condo price:** roughly **$1.1M – $1.4M** depending on size and condition

-   **Single-family homes:** often **$2M+**, with larger historic homes significantly higher

-   **Average price per square foot:** about **$1,100/sq ft**

Homes typically sell quickly when priced correctly, and limited inventory keeps demand relatively strong for well-located properties.

### **Market Dynamics**

-   Most homes in the neighborhood were built around the early 1900s and feature classic San Francisco architecture.

-   Inventory tends to be limited, with only a small number of listings available at any given time.

-   Buyers are often drawn by the neighborhood’s central location and access to transit and surrounding districts.

### **Lifestyle in Duboce Triangle**

The neighborhood offers a mix of quiet residential streets and easy access to lively nearby areas.
