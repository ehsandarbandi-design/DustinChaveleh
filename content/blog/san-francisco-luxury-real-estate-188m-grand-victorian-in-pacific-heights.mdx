**An Iconic Victorian Estate Hits the Market in Pacific Heights**

A rare Grand Victorian at 1948 Pacific Avenue has officially listed at **$18.8 million** — one of San Francisco's most notable luxury offerings this year.

Originally purchased in 2014 for approximately $5 million, the circa-1887 residence underwent a comprehensive five-year renovation that preserved its historic architectural character while delivering fully modern living. The result is 8,485 square feet across multiple levels, with six bedrooms and eight bathrooms.

**The Architecture**

Designed by architect Charles Kenitzer, the home is a showcase of Victorian craftsmanship — original double entry doors, 13-foot ceilings, wide-plank oak flooring, and a dramatic central staircase. A private elevator services the top four levels.

**The Amenities**

The lower level is built for entertaining: a lounge with a stage, wine cellar, and private gym. Outdoors, an al fresco kitchen opens to expansive terrace space with panoramic views of the city skyline and the Transamerica Pyramid. A three-car garage with gated driveway access rounds out a package that's genuinely rare for Pacific Heights.

The home has hosted high-profile private events — most recently a Super Bowl weekend activation for the Jordan Brand.

**The Location**

Moments from Union Street, Fillmore Street, and the Presidio — some of the city's best dining, shopping, and outdoor recreation.

*Represented by Anne Laury of Sotheby's International Realty and Joel Goodrich of Coldwell Banker Residential Brokerage.*
