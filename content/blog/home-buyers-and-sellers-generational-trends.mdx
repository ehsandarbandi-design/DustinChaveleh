Buying a first home isn't easy, but younger buyers are finding smart ways onto the property ladder. According to the latest [**National Association of REALTORS®**](https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics) **(NAR) Generational Trends Report**, 74% of buyers finance their purchase, leveraging strategic savings, family support, and specialized buyer programs to reach their homeownership goals.

In a market like **San Francisco**, utilizing local assistance can make all the difference. Key programs available to help first-time SF buyers include:

-   **Downpayment Assistance Loan Program (**[**DALP**](https://www.sf.gov/apply-downpayment-loan-buy-market-rate-home)**):** Offers up to $500,000 in deferred, silent-second down payment assistance for market-rate homes. Lottery Based Program.
    
-   **City Second Loan Program (**[**CSLP**](https://www.sf.gov/data--city-second-loan-program-cslp)**):** Provides silent-second down payment loans to help buyers purchase market-rate properties.
    
-   **Teacher Next Door (**[**TND**](https://www.sf.gov/reports--august-2024--about-teacher-next-door-program)**):** Tailored grants and low-interest loans to help SFUSD educators buy homes locally.
    
-   **Mortgage Credit Certificate (**[**MCC**](https://www.sf.gov/reports--december-2024--mortgage-credit-certificate)**):** Converts a portion of your annual mortgage interest into a direct tax credit to increase overall buying power.
