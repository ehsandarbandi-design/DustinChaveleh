# Reference Site

- **URL:** https://strade-fluid-demo.squarespace.com/  (Squarespace "Strade" template demo — a cycling tour company)
- Screenshots: add yours to `reference/screenshots/`
- Use it for **mood, typography and pacing only**. The content, layout and brand are not ours.

## What makes it feel expensive (measured from the live site)

**Typography**
- Inter everywhere, mostly **Light (300)** for large paragraphs and **Regular (400)** for headings. Almost no bold.
- **Negative letter spacing** on everything: headings about −6% (e.g. 46px H1 with −2.76px), cards about −6%, body about −2%.
- Large "statement" paragraphs (≈21–27px, Light) instead of classic headlines.
- Tight line height on headings (≈1.1), normal on body (≈1.25).
- Small **uppercase section labels in brackets**: `[ ABOUT ]`, `[ DESTINATIONS ]`, `[ TESTIMONIALS ]`, `[ FAQ ]`. (The demo sets these in a mono font — we do the same, with our **Monospace** style: Inconsolata Light 14px, uppercase.)
- A tiny **solid square ■** used as a marker next to labels and quotes.

**Color**
- Off-white page `#FBFBFB`, warm near-black text `#292826`, warm stone section `#E3E0DB`, muted taupe `#939085` for secondary text.
- No bright accent color. Color comes from the photography.

**Layout and pacing**
- Full-bleed photo/video hero with text over it, left-aligned (not centered).
- Very tall sections with lots of empty space; one idea per screen.
- Content cards are photos first, text small underneath (location line → title → short text).
- Sharp corners everywhere (border-radius 0). No shadows.
- Buttons: flat rectangles, uppercase small text, a small "↳" arrow before the label.

**Details**
- A live clock + date + city in the hero ("22:59:22 NEW YORK, NY / THURSDAY, 6.05.25") — a small editorial touch. For Dustin this could be the live San Francisco time.
- Section order: Hero → About statement → full-bleed image → Featured items → Statement → Founder → Testimonials → FAQ → Contact form → Closing statement + image.

## Borrow
- The light-weight Inter with negative tracking
- Bracketed uppercase labels and the ■ marker
- Left-aligned statement paragraphs over full-bleed imagery
- Warm off-white / stone / near-black palette feel, sharp corners, no shadows
- Slow pacing, generous section height
- The live local-time detail (as "San Francisco, CA")

## Do NOT copy
- Its text, images, logo, or the "Strade" brand
- Its exact section structure — we follow Dustin's page structure (see `CLAUDE.md`)
- Its exact fonts and sizes — we use our own type sheet (Inter + Inconsolata, 8 styles)
- The Squarespace promo badge and template-specific widgets
